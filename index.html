Robotics Researcher


CV | Github | Email


I am a robotics researcher focusing on SLAM, multi-sensor fusion, sensor calibration, and vision-language-action (VLA).

My work centers on engineering and integrating end-to-end robotic systems — from multi-sensor calibration pipelines to ROS-based software architectures for perception and mapping.

I am currently an M.S. student in Information and Communication Engineering at Hanbat National University (since Mar. 2026), where I work as a Graduate Researcher at the HBNU AiR Lab, advised by Prof. Dong-Geol Choi.

I received my B.S. in Electronics and Control Engineering, along with a double major in Advanced Sensor Convergence Device, from the same university (Feb. 2026), and continued at the AiR Lab as an Undergraduate Researcher throughout my studies.
